ABOUT THE CAMP

Working together.
Giving together.

The Working Camp is a mission of love and service organized by St. Mary Church – Faggala.

We gather to make beds, desks, drawers, food, and more, and deliver them to families in need for free.

It’s not just work… it’s worship in action.

WHAT WE DO

Hands that serve.
